01 — The problem

Voicemail is where
jobs go to die.

When a homeowner’s AC dies at 8 PM, they don’t leave a message and wait. They call the next company on Google. The job is gone in the ninety seconds your phone rings out.

It isn’t an effort problem — your office can’t be staffed at midnight, and your techs can’t answer from a crawlspace. It’s a coverage problem. And coverage is exactly what software is good at.

  • After hours

    Evenings, weekends and holidays are when breakdowns happen — and when most shops are dark. Every one of those calls currently ends at a recording.

  • Mid-job

    Peak season means every tech is on a roof or under a house. The office line rolls to voicemail precisely when call volume is highest.

  • Overflow

    Two calls at once means one caller hears a busy signal or a ring that never ends. They don’t call back. They call your competitor.

05 — Questions

Asked by owners like you.

Will my customers know it’s an AI?

Yes — and that’s deliberate. Your receptionist introduces itself as your virtual assistant. Callers don’t mind who answers; they mind whether someone answers, understands the problem, and books the job. Honesty also keeps you on the right side of disclosure rules in the US and EU.

What happens when a caller needs a human?

The call transfers to you, your office, or your on-call technician — based on rules you set. Emergencies, upset callers, and anything outside the script are escalated immediately, with a summary texted to you either way.

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. We connect to your existing business line and carrier. Your number, your Google listing, your trucks — nothing changes for your customers except that the phone always gets answered.

How long does setup take?

Most shops are live within a week of the kickoff call. We train the AI on your services and rules, test it against real call scenarios with you, and only go live when you approve how it sounds.

Is there a long-term contract?

No lock-in. The service runs month to month after setup. If it isn’t booking you jobs, you shouldn’t be paying for it — and you won’t be trapped into it.
